## Default markup

An empty state should say what happened and what to do next. The default markup has an icon, a title, a short text and an action button.

<Example>
<div class="empty">
  <div class="empty-icon"><Icon name="mood-sad" /></div>
  <p class="empty-title">No results found</p>
  <p class="empty-subtitle text-secondary">Try adjusting your search or filter to find what you're looking for.</p>
  <div class="empty-action">
    <a href="#" class="btn btn-primary"> <Icon name="search" /> Search again </a>
  </div>
</div>
</Example>

## Empty state with illustration

Replace the icon with an illustration when the empty state fills a whole page and there's room for a larger picture.

The illustrations in the examples come from [Tabler Illustrations](https://tabler.io/illustrations).

<Example>
<div class="empty">
  <div class="empty-img"><Illustration image="boy-with-key" alt="Empty state illustration" /></div>
  <p class="empty-title">Invoices are managed from here</p>
  <p class="empty-subtitle text-secondary">Try adjusting your search or filter to find what you're looking for.</p>
  <div class="empty-action">
    <a href="#" class="btn btn-primary"> <Icon name="plus" /> New invoice </a>
  </div>
</div>
</Example>

## Empty state with header

You can also leave out the icon or illustration and show only the text:

<Example>
<div class="empty">
  <div class="empty-header">404</div>
  <p class="empty-title">Oops… You just found an error page</p>
  <p class="empty-subtitle text-secondary">Try adjusting your search or filter to find what you're looking for.</p>
  <div class="empty-action">
    <a href="#" class="btn btn-primary"> <Icon name="arrow-left" /> Take me home </a>
  </div>
</div>
</Example>

## Accessibility

- The empty state title is the first thing users read, so make it a real heading at the right level for the page, not a styled `div`.
- Say what happened and what to do next. "No results" alone leaves users stuck; "No results for `tabler`. Try a shorter search." does not.
- The illustration is decoration. Give it `alt=""`, or `aria-hidden="true"` when it is an inline SVG.
- The action in `empty-action` should be a real `button` or link, so it can be reached with the keyboard.
- When the empty state replaces content after a search, announce it from a live region - otherwise a screen reader user gets no signal that the list changed.
