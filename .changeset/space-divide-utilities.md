Added `.space-x-*`, `.space-y-*`, `.divide-x`, `.divide-y` utilities (with `-0` and responsive variants) and a `child-selector` option for the utilities API. They replace the old `.space-*` (flex with `gap`) and `.divide-*` (border with padding) classes, which now space and separate the direct children of the container with `margin` and `border` only.
